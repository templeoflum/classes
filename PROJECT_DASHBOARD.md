## What we are making

A music-production map explored through Ableton. It helps someone do something, notice a relationship, find useful language, and return to an idea. It is not a required course, homework portal, or mastery tracker.

For the first student, it starts with **“I've just opened Live.”** It respects her existing musicianship and does not require learning the Atlas before making sound.
